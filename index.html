Jacob Gifford

Ph.D. Student in Economics
School of Economic Sciences, Washington State University

I am an economist working on water economics and policy. My research studies agricultural water allocation and markets, environmental flows, reservoir operations under uncertainty, groundwater, and the institutions that govern water in the Pacific Northwest.

My dissertation, Efficiency, Uncertainty, and Institutions in Environmental Water Resources Management, is advised by Jonathan Yoder (Chair), Michael Brady, and Kirti Rajagopalan.
